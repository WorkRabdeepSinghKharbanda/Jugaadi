## From responses to booking, without a middleman

Post what you need — skill, dates, rough location, optional daily wage — and nearby workers who match can respond. You review who's responded (sorted by distance, with their verification status), and book the one you want.

## What happens automatically

The moment you book, every other worker who responded to that task is cleared — no manual rejecting one by one. The task moves to "booked" status, and the worker's phone number appears so you can coordinate start time and location directly.

## Closing the loop

Once the work's done, mark the task complete. That's the full lifecycle: open → booked → completed (or cancelled, if you need to withdraw it). No ratings, no chat, no in-app payment yet — just enough to get someone booked fast.
