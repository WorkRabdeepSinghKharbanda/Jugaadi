## From posting to hiring, without a middleman

Post what you need — skill, dates, rough location, optional daily wage — and nearby workers who match can apply. You review who's applied (sorted by distance, with their verification status), and hire the one you want.

## What happens automatically

The moment you hire, every other applicant on that job is closed out — no manual rejecting one by one. The job moves to "hired" status, and the worker's phone number appears so you can coordinate start time and location directly.

## Closing the loop

Once the work's done, mark the job complete. That's the full lifecycle: open → hired → completed (or cancelled, if you need to pull a posting). No ratings, no chat, no in-app payment yet — just enough to get someone hired fast.
