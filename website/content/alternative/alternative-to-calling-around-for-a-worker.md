## The usual way: calling contacts, or the labour corner

Most shop owners and households solve a sudden staffing gap the same way they always have — calling through a list of past contacts hoping someone's free, or sending someone to the local labour corner and hoping for the best on quality and reliability.

## What's actually slow about it

Calling around means repeating the same conversation with each person until someone says yes — no way to see who's actually free without asking one at a time. A labour corner shows you who showed up that morning, not who's verified or what they're specifically good at.

## What Jugaadi changes

Post once, with the skill and dates you need. See nearby workers who've listed that skill and carry a pilot-verification badge, sorted so you can compare before deciding — instead of finding out person by person.

## What doesn't change

Jugaadi doesn't replace judgement — you still decide who to hire based on what you see, same as asking around. It also doesn't guarantee coverage; a single-city pilot means some areas have more workers signed up than others.
