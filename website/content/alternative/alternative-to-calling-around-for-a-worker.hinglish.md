## Aam tarika: contacts ko call karna, ya labour chowk

Zyada tar shop owners aur ghar achanak staff ki kami ko hamesha ki tarah solve karte hain — purane contacts ki list par call karke umeed karna ki koi free ho, ya kisi ko local labour chowk par bhejke quality aur reliability par kismat azmaana.

## Yeh actually slow kyun hai

Idhar-udhar call karne ka matlab hai har banda se wahi conversation repeat karna jab tak koi haan na kahe — ek-ek karke poochhe bina yeh jaanne ka koi tarika nahi ki actually kaun free hai. Labour chowk aapko dikhata hai ki us subah kaun aaya, yeh nahi ki kaun verified hai ya kis kaam mein achha hai.

## Jugaadi kya badalta hai

Ek baar post karo, jo skill aur dates chahiye unke saath. Paas ke workers jinhone woh skill list ki hai aur pilot-verification badge rakhte hain, dikhte hain — taaki aap ek-ek karke pata lagaane ke bajaye decide karne se pehle compare kar sako.

## Kya nahi badalta

Jugaadi aapki samajh ki jagah nahi leta — aap phir bhi jo dekhte ho uske basis par decide karte ho kise hire karna hai, jaise poochtaach karne mein karte ho. Yeh coverage ki guarantee bhi nahi deta; ek single-city pilot ka matlab hai kuch areas mein doosron se zyada workers sign up hain.
