## Running a shop with no backup staff

Most small shops run on one or two people. A sick day, a sudden rush of stock to unload, or an extra delivery run can be more than one person can handle, and finding a reliable hand on short notice usually means asking around the market.

## Post it, see who's nearby, hire

Post the skill you need and the dates. Workers nearby who've listed that skill — loading/unloading, delivery, packing, or general helper — can respond. You pick who to hire; their contact is shared once you do, and you pay them directly.
