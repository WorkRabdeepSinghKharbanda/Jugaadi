## When your regular help is unavailable

Most households rely on one person for cleaning, cooking, or general help. When they're sick, travelling, or leave, there's no backup plan — just asking neighbours or going without.

## How it works for households

Post what you need (cleaning, cooking, or general help) and the dates. Nearby pilot-verified workers who've listed that skill can apply; you pick who to hire and their phone number is shared once you do.

## A few things to check yourself

Jugaadi's verification is a manual pilot-stage check, not a background check or police verification. For household work, meet the person, ask for ID, and trust your own judgement the same way you would with any new hire — Jugaadi shortens the search, it doesn't replace common sense.
