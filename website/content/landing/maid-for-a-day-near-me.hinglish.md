## Jab aapka regular helper available nahi hai

Zyada tar ghar cleaning, cooking, ya general help ke liye ek banda par depend karte hain. Jab woh sick ho, travel par ho, ya chhod de, toh koi backup plan nahi hota — bas neighbours se poochna ya bina kaam ke rehna.

## Gharon ke liye yeh kaise kaam karta hai

Batao aapko kya chahiye (cleaning, cooking, ya general help) aur dates. Paas ke pilot-verified workers jinhone woh skill list ki hai, respond kar sakte hain; aap decide karte ho kise hire karna hai, aur hire karte hi unka phone number share ho jaata hai.

## Khud check karne laayak kuch baatein

Jugaadi ka verification ek manual pilot-stage check hai, background check ya police verification nahi. Household kaam ke liye, banda se milo, ID maango, aur apni samajh par bharosa karo jaise kisi bhi naye hire ke liye karte ho — Jugaadi bas search ko chhota karta hai, common sense ki jagah nahi leta.
