date: "2026-10-09"

Every small shop and household relies on one or two regular workers. When
someone calls in sick or leaves suddenly, there's rarely a fast way to find a
trustworthy replacement for just a day or two.

Jugaadi connects shop owners and households with verified workers nearby, for
tasks from a single day to a week. Post what you need, see who's available
close by, and book directly — no agency, no long wait.

We're starting with a single-city pilot to get the core experience right
before expanding further.
