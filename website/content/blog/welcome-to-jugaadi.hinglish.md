Har chhoti shop aur ghar ek ya do regular workers par depend karta hai. Jab koi sick ho jaaye ya achanak chhod de, toh sirf ek din ya do ke liye reliable replacement dhoondne ka koi fast tarika shayad hi hota hai.

Jugaadi shop owners aur gharon ko paas ke verified workers se jodta hai, ek din se ek hafte tak ke tasks ke liye. Aapko jo chahiye post karo, dekho paas mein kaun available hai, aur directly book karo — koi agency nahi, koi lamba wait nahi.

Hum ek single-city pilot se shuru kar rahe hain taaki aage badhne se pehle core experience sahi ho.
