English/Hindi/Hinglish for every screen. `lang.dart`: `LangController` (cached in shared_preferences, synced from `profiles.lang` after `/profile/me`) + `LangScope`. `strings.dart`: key→{en,hi,hinglish} map, `context.tr`, and `errorMessage(context, e)` (ApiException message / translated LocationException / generic). New user-facing text needs a key with all three languages.
